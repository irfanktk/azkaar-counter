Privacy Policy – Azkaar Counter

Effective date: 3 October 2026
Developer: IrfanUllah Khattak
Contact: engr.irfanktk@gmail.com

Azkaar Counter ("the app") helps you count the azkaar (remembrances) you recite after the five daily prayers. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking by the developer.
  • Everything you do in the app is stored only on your device.
  • The app has no internet permission and does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your phone, only so that the app works:

  • your azkaar lists (order, target counts and today's progress),
  • images you add by scanning a page or choosing a photo (saved privately inside the app),
  • your settings (beep, vibration, keep-screen-awake, theme, daily reset time).

This data never leaves your device through the app. The developer cannot see it.

Camera and photos

  • Scan document opens Google's document scanner, provided by Google Play services. The app does not request the camera permission itself.
  • Choose from gallery uses the Android Photo Picker. The app only receives the one photo you pick and does not request storage permission.
  • The image you crop is saved privately inside the app. The original photo in your gallery is not changed.

Google Play services (document scanner)

The document scanner is part of Google's ML Kit and runs inside Google Play services, not inside this app. According to Google, ML Kit may collect limited technical information (such as device model, Android version, app package name and version, per-installation identifiers and performance metrics) for diagnostics and usage analytics. It is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See also: ML Kit data disclosure.

If you never use Scan document, the scanner is not started.

Backups

If you have turned on Android backup for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup (for example, in your Google account). This is controlled by your device settings, not by the app.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete all data erases all lists, images, progress and settings stored by the app.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your device.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete on a server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for light vibration when you count and when a zikr is complete.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, phone, SMS or internet access.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
